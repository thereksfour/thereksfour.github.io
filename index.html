Approach

Manual review focused on concrete exploit paths.

01

Understand the system

Map the protocol's intended economic model, actors, privileged paths, integrations, and critical state transitions.

02

Define invariants

Identify the accounting, solvency, permission, oracle, and lifecycle assumptions that must remain true.

03

Validate exploitability

Prioritize material risks and back high-impact findings with concrete reasoning, PoCs, or invariant tests where appropriate.

04

Support remediation

Deliver concise findings with root cause, impact, recommended fixes, and fix review support when included in scope.
